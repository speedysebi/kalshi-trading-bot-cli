# Production readiness for the Bun/TypeScript Kalshi trading CLI

Research snapshot: 2026-11-11. Scope is the checked-out repository plus Kalshi's current primary documentation. “Implemented-looking” means source code contains a mechanism; it is not evidence that the deployed bot, account, data, or strategy is production ready. No live orders, credentials, account records, or integration results were inspected.

## 1. What would validate the strategy and point-in-time backtest?

### Takeaway
The repository has useful scoring and anti-look-ahead guards, but its historical P&L is still a signal study until the data, universe, fillability, costs, and out-of-sample performance are independently verified.

### Cited Findings
- Implemented-looking: the backtest selects an Octagon snapshot captured on or before a target date, rejects stale snapshots, uses per-contract probabilities and snapshot volume, and exposes missing-volume drops. Resolved P&L assumes entry at `market_probability`; unresolved P&L marks to current market price. [Snapshot selection](../../src/backtest/fetcher.ts); [backtest scoring](../../src/commands/backtest.ts).
- Implemented-looking: metrics include Brier comparisons, event-cluster bootstrap intervals and zero-skill baselines; the fee option defaults to `none`, and the taker option applies a simplified entry fee formula. [Metrics](../../src/backtest/metrics.ts); [fee calculation](../../src/commands/backtest.ts); [fee display](../../src/backtest/renderer.ts).
- The Octagon history fetcher requires `OCTAGON_API_KEY` and may return a subscription-required error. It caches historical model snapshots locally. These facts do not establish source timestamp semantics, past model versions, or complete coverage. [Fetcher](../../src/backtest/fetcher.ts).
- Kalshi separates live and historical data and publishes per-type cutoff timestamps. Older markets, candles, trades, orders, and positions may require historical endpoints; a single live endpoint cannot establish a complete historical universe. [Kalshi historical-data guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data.md).
- Kalshi's fee page shows standard and non-standard series fees and some maker fees, so one constant fee assumption cannot cover every traded market. [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule).

### Inferences
- **Gate: reproducible point-in-time dataset.** Freeze a manifest of event/market universe, contract rules, model version, input publication times, Octagon capture/availability times, Kalshi quote/trade times, fees and code commit. Require every signal to use data that was *available to the bot* before its decision, not merely timestamped before settlement. Sample a set of decisions and replay them from archived raw inputs. This follows from the stored snapshot selection and Kalshi's moving archive cutoffs. [Snapshot selection](../../src/backtest/fetcher.ts); [Kalshi historical-data guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data.md).
- **Gate: executable P&L.** Reprice entries against contemporaneous bid/ask and depth, model partial/no fills, queue position, latency, spread, slippage, cancellations, adverse selection, series-specific fees, and settlement. Report gross and net P&L separately. The current `market_probability` entry and simplified fee assumptions are insufficient evidence of realized returns. [Backtest scoring](../../src/commands/backtest.ts); [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule); [Kalshi order queue position](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/orders/get-order-queue-position.md).
- **Gate: selection proof.** Pre-register candidate rules and thresholds; use chronological train/validation/test windows, an untouched recent holdout, event-cluster uncertainty, calibration and net-return baselines, sensitivity to fees and fill rate, and breakdowns by series, period, price band and liquidity. Count all discovered, eligible, missing-history and dropped markets to expose survivorship and coverage bias. Existing baselines and clustered intervals provide pieces of this evidence but do not replace a holdout. [Metrics](../../src/backtest/metrics.ts); [Discovery](../../src/backtest/discovery.ts); [backtest scoring](../../src/commands/backtest.ts).
- **Gate: contract semantics.** Store the actual full contract rules and their effective version alongside the decision; test unusual settlements, early closes, voids or rule changes against the strategy's assumptions. Kalshi says each event contract has its own trading-period, payout and determination terms, and that a rules summary is not the full text. [Kalshi member agreement](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-member-agreement.pdf); [Kalshi rules-summary help](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823823-rules-summary).

### Gaps
- No verified Octagon historical feed, license/retention terms, point-in-time source availability, completeness report, recorded quote/depth tape, or untouched out-of-sample results were supplied. The backtest code alone cannot establish positive expected net returns. [Fetcher](../../src/backtest/fetcher.ts); [backtest scoring](../../src/commands/backtest.ts).

## 2. What staged release, execution reconciliation, and risk controls are needed?

### Takeaway
The CLI has a paper ledger, demo/live switch, V2 order path, hard per-order mandate, and settlement sync. Readiness still requires an exchange-authoritative order/fill ledger, fail-closed risk checks, and staged evidence from paper through capped live trading.

### Cited Findings
- Implemented-looking: paper positions are inserted at a requested price and later scored from Kalshi market results; the paper ledger explicitly places no exchange order. Its displayed P&L uses flat-bet economics and does not include a demonstrated fill or fee model. [Paper ledger](../../src/commands/paper.ts).
- Kalshi offers separate demo and production environments with separate credentials and mock funds; the code switches base URL via `KALSHI_USE_DEMO`. [Kalshi API environments](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_environments.md); [Kalshi demo guide](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823775-creating-and-using-a-demo-account); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts).
- Implemented-looking: V2 order building supports `client_order_id`, but the argument is optional; order placement enforces per-order mandate checks. The API client retries 429/5xx responses, including POST, with bounded backoff. A timed-out or ambiguous order submission is not reconciled in this function before a retry. [V2 orders](../../src/tools/kalshi/orders-v2.ts); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts).
- Implemented-looking: the mandate has a kill switch, per-order count and notional caps, and a daily realized-loss cap from local settlements. On DB failure, its daily-loss calculation returns zero. The risk gate allows a first trade with no drawdown snapshot. [Mandate](../../src/risk/mandate.ts); [risk gate](../../src/risk/gate.ts).
- Implemented-looking: settlement sync pages through live `portfolio/settlements`, reports an incomplete page-cap result, and closes matching local position rows. Kalshi says this endpoint excludes archived settlement records; positions and fills have their own historical behavior. [Settlement sync](../../src/tools/kalshi/settle.ts); [Kalshi settlements endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-settlements.md); [Kalshi positions endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-positions.md); [Kalshi fills endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-fills.md).
- Kalshi provides authenticated real-time user-fill notifications with unique `trade_id`, `order_id`, and optional `client_order_id`, and REST endpoints for orders, fills, positions, balance and settlements. [User fills](https://docs.kalshi.com/websockets/user-fills.md); [Get orders](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/orders/get-orders.md); [Get positions](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-positions.md).

### Inferences
- **Gate 0 — offline:** pass deterministic backtest replay and risk/order state-machine tests, including stale data, fees, partial fills, unknown submit outcome, restart, 429/5xx, cancellation races and settlement corrections. [Backtest scoring](../../src/commands/backtest.ts); [Kalshi create order V2](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/orders/create-order-v2.md).
- **Gate 1 — shadow paper:** run decisions against live timestamps and quotes without submitting orders; write intent, quote age, simulated fill rule, reject reason and eventual result to an append-only ledger. Compare paper P&L with achievable displayed prices and fees. The current paper ledger records the requested entry price, so this is additional evidence. [Paper ledger](../../src/commands/paper.ts); [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule).
- **Gate 2 — Kalshi demo:** exercise credential isolation, V2 order creation, partial fills, cancels, restarts, polling/WebSocket gaps, settlement and end-of-day reconciliation with mock funds. Keep demo results separate from production performance because credentials and funds are separate. [Kalshi API environments](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_environments.md); [Kalshi demo guide](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823775-creating-and-using-a-demo-account).
- **Gate 3 — capped live:** begin with a precommitted small order/notional/daily-loss/aggregate-exposure budget and manual review of every exception. Increase only after complete reconciliations, stable net results, and a tested halt/restart runbook. Risk caps should include outstanding orders and all exchange positions, not only local open-position rows. [Mandate](../../src/risk/mandate.ts); [Risk gate](../../src/risk/gate.ts); [Kalshi positions endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-positions.md).
- **Execution invariant:** persist one durable, unique intent ID before POST; map it to `client_order_id`, exchange order ID, every fill `trade_id`, cancel/amend, position and settlement. On an unknown response, query exchange state before any resubmit; never infer fill from order acceptance. Reconcile after every event and on startup from paginated exchange orders, fills, positions, balance and settlements, crossing live/historical cutoffs as needed. Halt new orders on mismatch, missing pages, stale market data or unavailable authoritative account state. [V2 orders](../../src/tools/kalshi/orders-v2.ts); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts); [User fills](https://docs.kalshi.com/websockets/user-fills.md); [Kalshi historical-data guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data.md).
- **Risk invariant:** fail closed if the DB, account balance, positions, risk snapshot, model data or exchange status cannot be verified; count gross worst-case exposure of resting orders plus held contracts; enforce per-event/series, aggregate, daily and drawdown caps at the final order chokepoint. Refresh risk from exchange state before order placement and test kill-switch persistence across restart. The current daily-loss fallback to zero and no-snapshot allowance make these explicit review targets. [Mandate](../../src/risk/mandate.ts); [Risk gate](../../src/risk/gate.ts); [Kalshi balance endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-balance.md).

### Gaps
- No exchange-account reconciliation report, complete intent-to-fill state machine, live/dry-run performance, or evidence of recovery from an ambiguous POST was provided. Optional client IDs and local settlement sync are insufficient to verify exactly-once *effects*. [V2 orders](../../src/tools/kalshi/orders-v2.ts); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts); [Settlement sync](../../src/tools/kalshi/settle.ts).

## 3. What monitoring, security, operations, audit, and Kalshi-rule checks close the release gate?

### Takeaway
Operational controls should make every order explainable and every uncertain state visible, while keeping keys isolated and honoring current Kalshi API and contract rules. Local logs and watchdog code are starting points, not proof of alert delivery, retention or compliance.

### Cited Findings
- Implemented-looking: the API client uses a 30-second request deadline, bounded exponential backoff and a dead-letter writer; audit events append JSON lines under `appPath('audit.jsonl')`. The watchdog checks local positions for edge convergence, adverse move, expiry and catalyst proximity. [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts); [audit trail](../../src/audit/trail.ts); [watchdog](../../src/scan/watchdog.ts).
- Kalshi's developer agreement requires bounded exponential-backoff retries, monitoring and alerting as needed, account security, responsiveness to API notices and cooperation with audits. It restricts API data caching/sharing to facilitating one's own trading and prohibits spoofing/wash sales, insecure transmission and other misuse. [Kalshi developer agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf).
- Kalshi's current rate-limit guide describes token-based read/write buckets, a separate balance bucket, and endpoint-specific costs; it advises reacting to fills/settlements/transfers rather than polling balance continuously. [Kalshi rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits.md).
- Kalshi documents separate API keys and recommends Ed25519 while supporting RSA-PSS. The checked-out client signs with RSA-PSS and accepts a PEM from an environment variable or file. [Kalshi API keys](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_keys.md); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts).
- Kalshi says each market's full rules determine outcome, source and timing; source-agency employees, holders of material nonpublic information and people able to influence outcomes can be prohibited from trading relevant contracts. [Kalshi market-rules help](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823822-market-rules); [Kalshi trading prohibitions](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/kalshi-source-agency-trading-prohibitions.pdf); [Kalshi member agreement](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-member-agreement.pdf).

### Inferences
- **Monitoring gate:** alert on stale model/market/clock data, API auth/rate/timeout errors, WebSocket gaps, DLQ growth, incomplete pagination, order/fill/position/settlement differences, limit breaches, unacknowledged cancels, unexpected fees and daemon failures. Measure detection and response time in a fault drill; route to a named operator with an escalation path. [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts); [Settlement sync](../../src/tools/kalshi/settle.ts); [Kalshi developer agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf).
- **Security gate:** keep demo/live keys and data directories distinct; use least-privilege API credentials where available, protected key files or a secret manager, rotation/revocation procedure, redacted logs, dependency and credential scans, and a production release review. Test the actual key algorithm and request signing against Kalshi's current documentation; the current client supports RSA-PSS only. [Kalshi API keys](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_keys.md); [API client](../../src/tools/kalshi/api.ts); [Kalshi API environments](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_environments.md).
- **Operations gate:** pin Bun/dependencies, run typecheck/unit and credentialed integration tests in a controlled environment, back up/restore SQLite and the audit log, exercise process restart and exchange outage, document cancel-all/kill-switch, and require a human owner for incident response and release rollback. Respect rate-limit buckets, monitor endpoint cost changes and API notices. [Package scripts](../../package.json); [Kalshi rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits.md); [Kalshi developer agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf).
- **Audit gate:** preserve immutable or tamper-evident records for model/input version, raw market/rules snapshot, decision and risk checks, intent, request/response identifiers, fills/fees, cancellations, reconciliations, settlement and operator override. Verify a sample trade can be reconstructed end to end and document retention/access controls consistent with Kalshi's agreement. The present JSONL append alone does not establish tamper evidence or backup. [Audit trail](../../src/audit/trail.ts); [Kalshi developer agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf).
- **Rules gate:** before enabling a series, review the current full contract rules, trading hours/status, fees, position limits and trader eligibility; block markets with unclear resolution terms or prohibited participation; recheck after rule/API notices. Keep a compliance review of self-trading/manipulative patterns and permitted API-data use. [Kalshi member agreement](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-member-agreement.pdf); [Kalshi trading prohibitions](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/kalshi-source-agency-trading-prohibitions.pdf); [Kalshi developer agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf); [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule).

### Gaps
- No operator rota, alert destination, SLOs, backup/restore proof, secret storage review, compliance sign-off, or audit retention policy was supplied. Exact current exchange rulebook PDF and account-specific position-limit/eligibility settings should be checked in Kalshi's live regulatory and account surfaces before live deployment. [Kalshi regulatory page](https://kalshi.com/regulatory/rulebook); [Kalshi member agreement](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-member-agreement.pdf).
